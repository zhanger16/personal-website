# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Recruiters screening candidates quickly. They are usually non-technical, arrive from a résumé, LinkedIn profile, or application link, and decide in under a minute whether the candidate moves forward. Their job is to confirm fit for a software engineering co-op placement and find a reason to pass the candidate along.

## Product Purpose
A personal website the owner links from job applications. It exists to make the owner memorable and easy to say yes to during a fast recruiter screen. Success means a recruiter quickly grasps who the owner is and what they've done, and then contacts them, downloads the résumé, or forwards the profile.

## Positioning
A memorable, confident personal site rather than a plain résumé page, without any sales or product framing (the owner explicitly dropped the "launch-style pitch" approach after a critique). Distinctiveness comes from craft; every claim stays backed by real evidence (projects, résumé, testimonials).

## Operating Context
- Visitors come from job applications, résumé links, and LinkedIn, on desktop and phone.
- The site sits alongside a résumé PDF; the résumé download is a primary action.
- Owner: Ryan Zhang, B.Sc. Computer Science at the University of British Columbia (Sep 2024 – expected May 2029), based in Vancouver, BC.
- Target roles: software engineering co-op placements (UBC Science Co-op). Evidence is co-op and part-time roles, research, teaching, coursework and projects.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static site with no build step: `index.html`, `assets/css/styles.css`, `assets/js/main.js`.
- Must work well on phones as well as desktop.
- Open: the co-op term being sought, availability date, work authorization, LinkedIn and GitHub URLs, and a third project are not yet recorded.

## Brand Commitments
- Unique yet professional (owner's original brief).
- No product or marketing metaphor (features, plans, changelog, checkout): the owner dropped it in favour of plain, evidence-first copy.
- Motion is purposeful and restrained: one authored moment, not effects scattered across the page.

## Evidence on Hand
- Résumé: November 2026 co-op résumé supplied in conversation; its content is on the site. The PDF itself is not yet at assets/resume.pdf.
- Projects: Neighbours in Need (nwHacks 2025, Node.js web app) and Blackjack (CPSC 210 project, extended). No links supplied yet.
- Real testimonials with permission to use: exist, not yet added.
- Professional headshot: planned by the owner, not yet available. The site should have a slot for it; until it arrives, use a clearly marked placeholder, never a stock or generated face.
- Do not invent metrics, employers, years of experience, user counts, or quotes. Every number and claim on the site must come from the owner.

## Product Principles
- Scannable in under a minute: the strongest evidence and the main actions come first.
- Evidence over adjectives: every claim points to a project, role, or quote.
- Honest for early career: present internships, school work, and projects with confidence, without inflating seniority.
- Personality that stays professional: memorable enough to stand out, never at the cost of clarity.
