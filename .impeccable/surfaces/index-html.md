# Surface: index.html (home)

Mode: Persuade. Recruiters screening an internship/new-grad candidate in under a minute, desktop and phone. Primary actions: download résumé, email. Proof: three real projects, experience/education, real testimonials (all owner-supplied; placeholders until then). Headshot planned.

Chosen direction: Suminagashi Ink Basin (user adopted a declined challenger). Memorable moment: a live ink basin you can stir, with the candidate's name set where the water meets the still paper.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a sheet of suminagashi paper: one live basin of indigo ink and a still margin of plain facts. It refuses the dark neon developer portfolio and the split hero with a card row.

OWN-WORLD: Paper-white water ground (#f6f7f9), indigo depth (#0a1a33) for ink, type and primary actions, dilute ink (#55627a) for secondary text, mist (#dde2e8) hairlines. Alegreya Sans, self-hosted: light display, regular body. No cards with icons, no gradients-as-chrome, no glow. Thin hairline rules, indigo solid primary button, outlined secondary.

STORY: Visitor sees who this is and what they want in one glance, trusts it because every claim points to a project, role or quote, then downloads the résumé or emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed WebGL basin behind the right ~60%; still paper margin left holding headshot slot, name in large light display crossing into the basin, one plain sentence (field, school, graduation, role sought), facts row (location, work authorization), Résumé (primary) and Email buttons. Phone: basin as top band, name overlapping its lower edge, sticky bottom Résumé/Email bar.

FORM: Suminagashi ink basin, catalog challenger medium-native-fluid-ink-basin, user-adopted; assigned list position 4 overridden by user choice. seed:0e59e9967ef1abf8

Signature interaction: pointer drags currents; a click drops fresh ink (newest drop darkest). Motion grammar: one opening drop that blooms; recency in experience shown by ink depth (newest darkest); project placeholders are prints pulled from the live basin. Reduced motion: a single still marbled frame.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
